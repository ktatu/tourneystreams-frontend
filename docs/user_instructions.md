# User instructions

This document will go over the features of the app, and how to use them.

## Adding (any) streams

https://github.com/ktatu/tourneystreams-frontend/blob/main/src/assets/siteguide_videos/step1_addstreams.mp4

Any Twitch or YouTube streams can be added from the app bar.

1. Select the platform (Twitch or YouTube) from the buttons on the left.
2. a: If adding a Twitch stream, type the channel name. The channel name just means the name of the streamer. Capitalization doesn't matter here.
3. b: If adding a YouTube stream, paste the YouTube stream url, e.g., https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=123456
4. Click the big button!

## Followed streams

https://github.com/ktatu/tourneystreams-frontend/blob/main/src/assets/siteguide_videos/step2_followed.mp4

This is where you can see a list of Twitch channels you follow that are currently streaming.

If you haven't connected your Twitch account to Tourneystreams before or your authentication has expired, you will see a button asking you to connect your Twitch account to Tourneystreams.

- If you have connected to Tourneystreams before and not removed that connection, clicking the button will simply re-authenticate you and redirect you to the page.
- If you haven't connected before, you will see the screen below, asking you to authorize Tourneystreams. The page will also state what the app will gain access to, which in this case is the list of the channels you follow.

## Presets

https://github.com/ktatu/tourneystreams-frontend/blob/main/src/assets/siteguide_videos/step3_presets.mp4

Presets is another convenience tool, allowing you to make groups of Twitch streamers, that you can open at once.

- You can edit presets by clicking the pen icon.
- A similar view, but for adding presets, opens from clicking the button on the top-right of the drawer tab.

## Streams in the app bar

Every open stream shows as an item in the app bar.

- When you have more than one stream open, you can change the display positions of streams by dragging an item from the pointing arrows icon.
- The comment icon open's the stream's chat. If you are logged into Twitch / YouTube, then you should be able to send messages.

## Settings

The settings tab opens from the top-left gear-icon. The possibly non-obvious setting is "hide stream thumbnails". It simply means that the preview images of streams aren't shown in the Followed and Presets tabs, which condenses the list.
